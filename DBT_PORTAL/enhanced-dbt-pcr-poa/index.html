Scheme Coverage

PCR Act Violations

Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955

  • Untouchability Practice (₹25,000-50,000)
  • Social Boycott (₹15,000-30,000)
  • Denial of Services (₹10,000-25,000)
  • Forced Labor (₹25,000-50,000)

PoA Act Atrocities

Scheduled Castes & Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989

  • Physical Assault (₹50,000-1,00,000)
  • Sexual Violence (₹1,00,000-5,00,000)
  • Property Damage (Actual + ₹25,000)
  • Economic Boycott (₹25,000-75,000)

Inter-caste Marriage Incentive

Dr. Ambedkar Scheme for Social Integration

  • Central Scheme Incentive (₹2,50,000)
  • State Additional Benefits (₹50,000-1,00,000)
  • Wedding Support Services
  • Rehabilitation Assistance
